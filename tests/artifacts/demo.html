How does sandbox isolation work?

The iframe has sandbox="allow-scripts allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox". Without allow-same-origin, the iframe gets a unique origin and cannot:

  • Read the parent's localStorage or cookies
  • Make same-origin fetches to Caco's /api/*
  • Access window.parent's DOM (DOMException on access)
